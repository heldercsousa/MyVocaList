Three styles — which feels right?

1

Slow pulse (top-right)

Fades in and out every ~2.5s. Calm, not distracting. Admin always knows the next singer. Text shadow only — no background.

2

Quick blink (bottom-left)

Hard on/off every ~1.2s. Grabs attention fast — useful when the singer needs to get up now. Ultra-minimal: just a name.

3

Fade in → hold → fade out (top-left)

Appears fully, stays readable for ~2s, then disappears for ~1s. Best balance: readable enough for the admin, gone most of the time for the singer.

Why this is better than the floating card

  • No positioning problem — even if it overlaps lyrics, it's gone in <1s
  • No AI needed — problem is solved by the blinking itself
  • Works on iOS too — it's just an animated Label in the overlay window
  • Readable on any background via text-shadow (dark halo around white text)
  • Implementation: one Label + one CSS-equivalent animation in MAUI
