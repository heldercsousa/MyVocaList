C — Local Notifications

App fires a local notification ~45s before the current song ends, then again at ~15s. Shows as a banner over YouTube. Simple, works on all iOS versions.

✅ Works on all iOS versions
✅ Also works on Android
✅ Low implementation complexity
⚠️ Requires notification permission
⚠️ Less precise than overlay

Recommended combination

Android: Blinking overlay label via SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW — as designed.
iOS (primary): A — AirPlay to TV, app stays in foreground. No extra code, works today.
iOS (enhancement): C — Local notifications as a fallback for when AirPlay isn't available.
iOS (future): B — Live Activity when there's capacity to implement ActivityKit.
