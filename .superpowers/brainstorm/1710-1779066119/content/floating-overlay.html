How it works technically

✅ Android

SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW permission — user grants once. App draws an overlay over any other app, including YouTube.

⚠️ iOS

iOS does not allow overlay windows over other apps. On iOS, this feature would not be available in Option C.

What the overlay shows

1

Minimal — next singer + countdown

Just the next singer's name and a "prepare" countdown. Small, unobtrusive.

2

Full — queue panel

Next 2–3 singers visible, round info, queue position. Admin has full context without switching apps.
