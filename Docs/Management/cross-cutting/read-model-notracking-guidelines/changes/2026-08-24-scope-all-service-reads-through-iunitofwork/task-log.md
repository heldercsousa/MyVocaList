### Origin

This item exists because the UoW Phase 4.6b audit conclusion was **wrong**, and the correction
is recorded in the parent spec's `task-log.md § CORRECTION 2026-08-24`. The rollout claim
"every service is inside the pattern" was reasoned per-**service** when it had to be per-**method**:
writes were migrated (Phases 4.1–4.5), reads were never in scope at all. BUG-078 came out of that
correction, and Helder chose the **broad** fix over the narrow one — "scope all reads to close 4.7" —
because `DbLoadGate` cannot be removed while reads still share the captive app-lifetime `AppDbContext`.

| # | Decision |

| D2 | The rationale is **DbContext lifetime** — NOT tracking (already globally `NoTracking`), NOT transactions (`ExecuteReadAsync` opens none). Recorded in `design.md § 2` as an explicit correction. |
| D3 | HTTP (and file/zip I/O) stays **outside** the unit of work. |
| D4 | Paged list reads are mandatory scope — they are what `DbLoadGate` serializes. |
| D5 | `DbLoadGate` removal needs both REQ-UOW-29 limbs; the `Task.Run` offloads must survive it. |
| D6 | `SongSuggestionService`/`ArtistSuggestionService` are **pre-built, not dead code** — destined for the `ArtistFormPage`/`SongFormPage` autocomplete feature. They get the pattern now; DI registration belongs to that future feature, so `MauiProgram.cs` appears in no wave. |

   breaks the two suggestion-service test files, but REQ-UOW-49 demanded a zero-diff over
   `MyVocaList.Tests/`. Fixed with a closed four-row carve-out table.
